## Question

Which acceptance checks, sandboxing measures and cache-isolation rules does Numina's Fuse use that
ADR-0008 and ADR-0011 lack, and the reverse?

## Why it matters

Fuse is the closest production system (R1). Differences point at checks or isolation rules one of
the two systems is missing.

## Method

A walkthrough by the owner with the Numina team, comparing their checks and isolation with the
gate stages in ADR-0011 and the sharing table in ADR-0008. Record only what may be shared.

## Exit criteria

- A list of stages and rules either system has that the other lacks.
- Proposed ADR amendments, drafted as new proposed ADRs.

## Time box

One meeting plus follow-up.
